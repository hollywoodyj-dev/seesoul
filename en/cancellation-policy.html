Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds

Draft counselling policy

This page is an unofficial English rendering of Chinese draft v1.1. It applies, once in force, to SeeSoul one-to-one counselling, online or face-to-face in Sydney.

Version: v1.1 · copy complete · pending legal and professional review
Revised 22 October 2026
Status: not yet in force. Not a basis for charging AUD $75 now. The Chinese page is the current public draft. This English page is not a separate contract.

Standard counselling fee: AUD $150 / 60 minutes.

Life Theme work (AUD $100 / 50 minutes) is not covered by this policy. Other arrangements apply there.

SeeSoul wants booking rules that respect both a client’s needs and time that has been set aside professionally.

Cancel and no-show fees described here are intended only for bookings already confirmed by SeeSoul, where the client was given and accepted the applicable terms before making the booking commitment, and only so far as the law allows.

Payment, booking confirmation and signed informed consent are separate. A successful payment does not confirm a time, and does not complete consent.

01｜Cancel or reschedule in advance

Please notify SeeSoul at least 24 hours before the confirmed session start.

Notice at least 24 hours ahead is intended to carry no cancel or reschedule fee.

  • Cancel: if AUD $150 has been paid, that session payment is in principle refunded in full.
  • Reschedule: a paid fee can be moved to a new time both sides agree. The new time still needs a separate confirmation from SeeSoul.

02｜Less than 24 hours’ notice

If a confirmed session is cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice, SeeSoul in principle intends to charge 50% of the standard fee, that is AUD $75.

That would apply only if the client was clearly told in advance, accepted valid terms, and the fee is reasonable under applicable law.

If AUD $150 has been paid and AUD $75 is confirmed after review, the remaining AUD $75 would be refunded.

If payment has not been made, SeeSoul would only seek a fee where terms are valid, accepted in advance, and lawful.

This is not an automatic penalty applied without review. Urgent or disputed situations are considered fairly on the facts.

03｜Not attending (no-show)

A no-show means the client did not attend at all during the whole confirmed slot.

Arriving late is not automatically a no-show.

If there is no valid cancel before the start, and the client does not attend for the whole slot, the intended fee is also 50% (AUD $75), subject to prior notice, valid terms, reasonableness and law.

A cancel received after the session has started, where the client never attends, is in principle reviewed as a no-show.

The same booking would not be charged both a late-cancel fee and a no-show fee.

If AUD $150 has been paid and AUD $75 is confirmed, the remaining AUD $75 is in principle refunded. Online and face-to-face sessions follow the same principle.

04｜Emergencies and special circumstances

Unforeseen difficulty can happen. If sudden illness, a family emergency or another serious unforeseen event means you cannot attend, contact SeeSoul as soon as you reasonably can.

SeeSoul will consider fairly, on the facts: waiving or reducing a fee; agreeing a new time; or another reasonable arrangement.

You do not need to put detailed sensitive health information in an ordinary cancel email. If more is needed, SeeSoul will say what minimum information is required and how to provide it.

05｜Lateness and technical problems

If you expect to be late, tell SeeSoul as early as you can. Remaining time in the booked slot is usually still available; the session is not automatically extended.

If you did attend, it is not treated as a full no-show.

For online sessions, contact SeeSoul quickly if the network, device or video platform fails, so a reasonable alternative can be tried. Technical failure is not automatically a no-show. If SeeSoul cannot deliver the agreed service, section 06 and applicable law apply.

06｜If SeeSoul cancels or reschedules

If SeeSoul cannot provide the session at the confirmed time, we will tell you as early as we can. You may choose a free move to a mutually agreed time, or cancel with a full refund of that session’s paid fee. This does not limit other rights you may have under law.

07｜Refunds

Where a refund is due, SeeSoul will arrange it in a reasonable time, in principle via the original payment path. Timing can vary by bank or platform. Statutory refund rights are not limited by this policy.

08｜How to cancel or reschedule

Use the SeeSoul contact given in the confirmation email. Notice time is in principle the time SeeSoul can verify receipt.

The free-cancel deadline is a full 24 hours counted back from the confirmed start, in Sydney time (AEST or AEDT as on that date). If daylight saving changes in between, elapsed time is used, not the same clock hour the day before. Notice received exactly at the deadline still counts as in time.

Confirmation emails are intended to list the date and start, the timezone, the free-cancel deadline, and how to contact SeeSoul.

09｜Fairness and consumer rights

This policy does not exclude, restrict or modify rights that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law and other applicable law.

Until it is formally adopted, legal and professional review, an effective date, and matching website, payment and confirmation wording still have to be completed. Confirmed bookings are handled under the terms given and accepted at the time, not automatically under a later website update.
