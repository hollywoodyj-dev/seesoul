Cancellation and rescheduling

Unofficial English rendering of draft policy v1.1. Not yet in force. Not a basis for charging AUD $75 at this stage. The Chinese page is the current public draft. This summary is not a separate contract.

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please notify SeeSoul at least 24 hours before the confirmed session start. Notice at least 24 hours ahead is intended to carry no cancel or reschedule fee.

Late cancel (under 24 hours), or not attending for the whole confirmed slot without cancelling, is drafted as AUD $75 (50% of the standard counselling fee). Whether that would apply depends on terms clearly given and accepted in advance, the facts, and applicable law.

Arriving late is not automatically a no-show. The same booking would not be charged both a late-cancel fee and a no-show fee. If something urgent or technical happens, contact SeeSoul.

This draft applies to one-to-one counselling, not Life Theme work.
