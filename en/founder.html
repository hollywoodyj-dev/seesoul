How counselling works Book a session

You may be here because something recently has left you tired, confused, or unsure how to continue.

You may already have tried many things — books, advice, efforts to improve — while a feeling, a relationship or a difficulty still returns.

I started SeeSoul so there could be a space where you do not have to look strong, and you do not have to arrive with a finished story. We can start with what you most want to talk about now.

How we work

What counselling with me is like

You do not need a complete life story for a first session. We usually begin with what matters most now, for example:

  • a relationship that is hard to be in
  • mood and pressure that have built up
  • tension between family expectations and your own choices
  • patterns you cannot quite shift
  • questions about yourself, direction or meaning

Counselling is not an exam. You do not need the right answer, and you do not need visible progress after every session.
